About the company

Texas BEB Investments Corporation is a Texas corporation founded in May 2012 by Brock E. Bruce, who leads it as Founder and CEO. The company is privately held and operates from its office on Capricorn Street in Stafford, Fort Bend County.

We own and run local service businesses ourselves. We don't just invest from a distance. We take a long-term view, keep management close to customers and crews, and invest in the equipment and people that let a business grow.

Prior investment

PROSHRED Houston Mobile onsite document shredding & secure destruction
Successful exit

Texas BEB Investments owned and operated PROSHRED Houston, with Brock Bruce as President and General Manager. The company provided mobile onsite paper shredding, hard drive and media destruction, product destruction and drop-off shredding for businesses across Greater Houston. BEB built the franchise from launch, grew revenue more than 20% a year, and expanded to a 13-vehicle fleet of shred trucks, box trucks and service vehicles. The business was named National Franchise of the Year in 2017, 2018, 2021 and 2023. In March 2026, BEB sold it to a private equity investor in a successful exit.

What is Texas BEB Investments Corporation?

Texas BEB Investments Corporation is a privately held corporation formed in Texas on May 22, 2012. It is headquartered in Stafford, Texas, and is led by its founder and CEO, Brock E. Bruce.

What companies does Texas BEB Investments own?

Texas BEB Investments Corporation currently owns HomeField Southwest Houston, a septic and onsite wastewater service company serving Fort Bend County, Brazoria County and southwest Greater Houston. It previously owned and operated PROSHRED Houston, a mobile document shredding company, which it built and then sold to a private equity buyer in 2026.

Who leads the company?

Texas BEB Investments Corporation is led by Brock E. Bruce, Founder and Chief Executive Officer; Emily N. Bruce, Vice President, Secretary and Treasurer; and Blake Gilbert, Vice President of Operations.

Where is the company located?

The company's office is at 12703 Capricorn St, Suite 100, Stafford, TX 77477, in Fort Bend County in the Greater Houston area.

Is the company in good standing?

Yes. Texas BEB Investments Corporation is registered with the Texas Secretary of State under file number 0801599899, and its registration and right to transact business in Texas are both active.
